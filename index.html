About Smilecare Dental Studio

Smilecare Dental Studio is dedicated to delivering dependable and professional oral healthcare to individuals and families in Bokamoso and surrounding Rustenburg communities.

Our approach combines patient comfort, cleanliness, and practical dental guidance to help you maintain a healthy smile with confidence.

Built on Care and Trust

Professional standards, hygienic practice, and a welcoming experience for every patient.

Our Services

Comprehensive care focused on everyday oral health and treatment needs.

Consultation & Examination

Detailed dental checks to assess oral health and recommend the right treatment plan.

Teeth Cleaning

Professional cleaning to remove buildup, freshen breath, and protect long-term oral health.

Fillings / Restorations

Repair and restore damaged teeth to improve function, comfort, and smile confidence.

Extractions

Gum Disease Treatment
